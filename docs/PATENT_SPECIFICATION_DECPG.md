# Technical Specification: Decentralized Edge-Cognitive Pricing Governance (DECPG) v2.0 (Ironclad)

**Date**: April 2026  
**Subject**: Systems and Methods for Securing Electronic Marketplaces via Federated Edge-Cognitive Behavioral Analysis and Neural Economic Fusion.

---

## 1. Field of the Invention
The present invention relates generally to the fields of **Distributed Artificial Intelligence**, **Cybersecurity**, and **Algorithmic Dynamic Pricing**. Specifically, it concerns the neutralization of automated agents (bots) in high-demand electronic marketplaces through real-time behavioral fingerprinting and decentralized neural network fine-tuning.

## 2. Abstract
A system and method for a **Decentralized Edge-Cognitive Pricing Governance (DECPG)** framework are disclosed. The system employs a localized neural network ("Edge-Sentinel") within a client-side environment to analyze human-computer interaction (HCI) patterns. These patterns are used to generate a "Cognitive Confidence Score" which is fused with global market variables via a central Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) to determine dynamic asset pricing. Furthermore, the system utilizes **Federated Learning** to aggregate locally learned behavioral weights into a global model through a secure, audited aggregation protocol, ensuring privacy-preserving evolution of threat detection.

## 3. Detailed Description of the Preferred Embodiments (Ironclad Enhancements)

### A. Spectral-Density Behavioral Validation
To neutralize "Slow-Motion" or "Temporal-Camouflage" bots, the Edge-Sentinel incorporates **Spectral-Density Thresholding**.
- **The Mechanism**: The system performs a frequency-domain analysis (Zero-Crossing Rate) on mouse coordinates.
- **The Principle**: Human skeletal-muscle interaction exhibits a natural tremor/vibration frequency between 2Hz and 10Hz.
- **Innovation**: Signals that are "Too Smooth" (Zero Jitter) or "Pure Noise" (Synthetic Jitter) are rejected by the Spectral-Density Gate, even if their spatial path appears valid.

### B. Context-Locked Cryptographic Signatures
To neutralize "Replay" or "Playback" attacks, the system implements **Session-Linked Nonce Injection**.
- **The Mechanism**: For every transaction, the central aggregator issues a unique, time-sensitive `sessionNonce`.
- **The Principle**: The final humanity proof is a cryptographic hash of the (Neural Score + Spectral Density + Session Nonce).
- **Innovation**: A behavioral proof generated in one session is mathematically invalid for any other session, preventing the scaling of recorded human movements.

### C. Reputation-Weighted Federated Aggregation
To neutralize "Sybil Poisoning" attacks on the global model, the system implements **Identity-Linked Governance**.
- **The Mechanism**: Incoming neural weights from federated nodes are weighted ($W$) based on the node's **Reputation Score** ($R$).
- **The Principle**: Nodes associated with long-standing accounts and verified purchase histories have a significantly higher influence on the global model's evolution than new or anonymous nodes.
- **Innovation**: This ensures that even a massive bot-swarm cannot "drift" the global human-behavior baseline.

### D. Topological Market Governance (Graph-AI)
The system incorporates a **Graph-Relational Database (Neo4j)** to model the "Market Topology."
- **The Mechanism**: Every transaction is mapped as a node-edge relationship between the `Purchaser`, the `DeviceID`, and the `Physical Seat ID`.
- **The Principle**: While individual bots may mimic human movement, their **Network Topology** (how they cluster around specific high-value seats or share underlying hardware signatures) is inherently different from organic human distribution.
- **Innovation**: The system performs a **Spatial-Cognitive Audit**, where the dynamic price is influenced by the "Clustering Coefficient" of suspicious nodes in the physical venue graph.
