## 🧠 Core Intelligence Architecture

### 1. Adaptive AI Pricing (Phase 6-8)
The platform features a multi-layer pricing engine that moves beyond static rules:
- **Sentiment-Aware Social Momentum**: Integrated with **Neo4j**, the system detects when a user's social circle is attending an event and applies a "FOMO" premium in real-time.
- **A/B Pricing Tournaments**: Multiple ML models compete simultaneously to find the highest-converting price points.
- **ROI Tracking**: Every price shift is logged to track the "AI Revenue Surplus"—the exact amount of profit generated by the intelligence layer.

### 🛡️ Ironclad Security (Phase 5-10)
Designed to survive the world's most aggressive flash sales:
- **Bot-Shield Sentinel**: Multi-layer inspection of headers, fingerprints, and behavioral velocity.
- **Proof-of-Humanity (Temporal Proof)**: A silent cryptographic PoW challenge that forces bots to waste CPU cycles, effectively stopping automated purchase scripts.
- **Adaptive Circuit Breakers**: The system "senses" downstream service latency and dynamically adjusts failure thresholds to prevent cascading crashes.

### 🚀 Performance & Scalability
- **Modular Monolith**: Clean domain-driven design that scales without the overhead of microservices.
- **Hot-Path Caching**: Redis-backed strategies for pricing and inventory that maintain <100ms response times under fire.
- **Materialized Projections**: Pre-aggregated sales data for instant admin reporting without expensive DB joins.
